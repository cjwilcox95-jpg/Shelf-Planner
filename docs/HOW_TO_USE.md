# How to use Shelf Planner

A guide for someone who has never seen it. No computer knowledge needed.

Open it on your phone: **https://cjwilcox95-jpg.github.io/Shelf-Planner/**

## Starting

- **Just looking?** Tap **Try it with demo products**. The products are made up, so you can't break anything.
- **Got a file for your store?** Tap **Choose the file** and pick it. It can be:
  - a **backup file** someone sent you (it has their plans and the store's products),
  - the store's product file, **catalog.json**,
  - or a spreadsheet of your products saved as **CSV**, with a UPC column and a Description column.
- The file stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. Costs and prices are never read.

## The five things to know

1. **Tap a shelf to work on it.** You start with a picture of the whole set. "Whole set" at the bottom takes you back.
2. **− and + change facings.** A facing is one product showing at the front. 3 facings means 3 side by side.
3. **Tap a product to move it.** Arrows move it left, right, up or down a shelf. Or press and hold it in the picture,
   then slide it where you want it.
4. **Scan or Add puts a product on** the shelf you are working on.
5. **The tag at the top says if it fits.** Green: everything fits. Amber: something is too tall or too deep.
   Red: a shelf is over. Tap the tag to see why.

## Good to know

- **It saves by itself** after every change, on your phone.
- **Undo** takes back the last change. **Redo** is under Tools.
- **Try a new set without losing the current one:** Plans, tap **⋯** on the plan, then **Copy**. Change the copy.
  **Tools > Compare** then lists what to move.
- **Keep a copy, or move to another phone:** Plans, then **Backup**, then **Export backup**. Send that file to the
  other phone and choose it on the first screen there.
- **Sizes start as guesses** (marked ~). To put in a real size: tap the product, **Details**, **Edit size**.
  Or **Tools > Measure as you go**: scan, type the size from your tape, scan the next.
- **Print:** Tools, then **Print or save as PDF**. You get a drawing to scale and a list to tick off.
- **No signal?** It still works once it has been opened with a signal.
- **Add it to your home screen** so it opens like an app: in Safari tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen"; in Chrome
  tap the ⋮ menu, then "Add to Home screen".

## If something goes wrong

- **A message says "Something went wrong":** your plans are safe. Tap **Open the app again**. If it keeps happening,
  tap **Save a backup file first** and send that file to whoever looks after the app.
- **"A newer version of the app is ready":** tap **Use it now**.
- **"This browser is not letting Shelf Planner save anything":** you are in a private window. Open the link in a
  normal window.
- **The camera won't scan:** allow the camera for the site in your phone's settings, or type the numbers under the
  barcode instead.

## What the words mean

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Facing | One product showing at the front of the shelf. |
| Section | One set of shelves, top to bottom. Usually 4 feet wide. |
| Aisle | Several sections side by side. |
| AFI set | A planogram from AFI. Start a plan from one and it comes with AFI's products and facings. |
| Room above | The space between a shelf and the shelf above it (also called clearance). |
| Off the shelf | Products that belong to the plan but are not on a shelf right now. |
| ~ and ≈ | ~ is a guessed size. ≈ is an estimate. No mark means it was measured. |
| Special order | A product the store can order but does not normally stock. |
